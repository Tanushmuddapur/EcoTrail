01 — OUR STORY

A journey towards better travel.

EcoTrail is a web project designed to make travel discovery more organised, engaging and conscious of the environment.

From discovering destinations to planning trips and saving favourite places, the platform brings useful travel features together in one place.

Beyond exploring destinations, EcoTrail encourages travellers to think about local communities, natural habitats and the impact of everyday travel choices.

02 — WHAT GUIDES US

Our purpose. Our promise.

Our approach brings together practical travel tools and a commitment to more thoughtful exploration.

Our Mission

To help people discover destinations, organise their journeys and make more informed choices about responsible travel.

Our Vision

To encourage a travel culture that appreciates nature, respects local traditions and values the places we visit.

Our Values

Creativity, accessibility, responsible exploration, useful technology and respect for the environment.

04 — OUR COMMITMENT

Better journeys.
A greener mindset.

Responsible travel is about making thoughtful choices before, during and after a journey. EcoTrail encourages visitors to consider how their decisions affect destinations and the people who call them home.

  • ✓ Reduce single-use plastic and litter.
  • ✓ Respect wildlife and natural habitats.
  • ✓ Support local businesses and communities.
  • ✓ Consider lower-impact transport options.
  • ✓ Respect local customs and traditions.
  • ✓ Leave natural spaces as you found them.
