Responsible-travel guidance
YOUR LIGHTER FOOTPRINT

Small choices make a difference.

  • Carry a reusable water bottle and avoid unnecessary single-use plastic.
  • Stay on marked trails and never disturb wildlife.
  • Use water and electricity thoughtfully.
  • Support local guides, artisans and independently operated businesses.
  • Take your waste with you and follow local disposal rules.
